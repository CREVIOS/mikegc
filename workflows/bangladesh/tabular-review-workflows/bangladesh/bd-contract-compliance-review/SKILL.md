# Contract Compliance Review (Bangladesh)

Review each contract for the points a Bangladeshi lawyer checks before signing or relying on it. Answer from the document only, quote the clause you rely on, and say "Not stated" when the document is silent. Documents may be in Bangla, English or both: quote them in their own language, and write amounts with lakh/crore grouping in ASCII digits.
