# Data Protection Review (Bangladesh)

Review the attached privacy notice, data processing agreement, contract clauses or description of a data flow under Bangladeshi law.

1. Read the current text first with the Laws of Bangladesh tools: the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 (ব্যক্তিগত উপাত্ত সুরক্ষা আইন, ২০২৬, Act No. 63 of 2026), which replaced the Personal Data Protection Ordinance, 2025, and, where relevant, the National Data Management Act, 2026, the Cyber Security Act, 2026, the Bangladesh Telecommunication Act, 2001 and sector rules (Bangladesh Bank for financial services). Note which provisions are not yet in force: some take effect only from a date to be set by notification.
2. Map the processing: who is the data fiduciary and who processes on its behalf, the categories of personal data (flag sensitive data such as biometric, health, financial, religious or political data, and children's data), purposes, legal basis, retention, recipients and any transfer outside Bangladesh.
3. Check against the Act: consent (voluntary, specific, informed, explicit and revocable, with the burden of proof on the fiduciary), notice, purpose limitation and minimisation, data subjects' rights, security measures, breach notification, audits, the classification of data, and the conditions for cross-border transfer and any data that must stay in Bangladesh. Where the Act leaves details to regulations that have not been published, say so rather than guessing.
4. Give a gap table: requirement, provision, what the document does, gap, fix, priority. Draft replacement clauses where the user wants them, in the document's language.
5. List open questions for the client and what must be verified once regulations are issued.
