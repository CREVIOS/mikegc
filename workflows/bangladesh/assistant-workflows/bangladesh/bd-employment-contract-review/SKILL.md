# Employment Contract Review (Bangladesh)

Review the attached appointment letter, employment contract or HR policy for compliance with Bangladeshi labour law.

1. Decide first whether the employee is a "worker" under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 (section 2(65) excludes people employed mainly in a managerial, administrative or supervisory capacity) or is otherwise outside the Act. Say what you assumed; the answer changes which protections apply.
2. For workers covered by the Act, check against the current text (read it with the Laws of Bangladesh tools, including the Bangladesh Labour (Amendment) Act, 2026):
   - appointment letter and identity card, and the service book (sections 5-6);
   - classification and probation (section 4);
   - working hours, rest, overtime at twice the ordinary rate (sections 100-108);
   - leave: casual, sick, annual, festival and maternity benefit (Chapters IV and IX);
   - termination by the employer and by the worker, notice or pay in lieu, misconduct procedure, discharge, dismissal, and the benefits due on each (sections 20-27);
   - wages, deductions and payment dates (Chapter X); provident fund, gratuity and workers' participation fund where they apply;
   - harassment and grievance procedures introduced or strengthened in 2026.
3. For anyone outside the Act, review the contract as a contract (Contract Act, 1872), including restraint clauses against section 27.
4. Flag terms that are unlawful, unenforceable, missing or unusual, with the clause and the provision. Do not invent minimum wage figures: the Minimum Wage Board sets them by sector, so tell the user to confirm the current gazette.
5. Give a table (clause, issue, provision, fix) and, if asked, tracked edits. Keep Bangla documents in Bangla.
