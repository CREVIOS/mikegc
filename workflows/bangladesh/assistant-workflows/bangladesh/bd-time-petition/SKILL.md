# Time Petition (Adjournment)

Draft an application for time (সময়ের দরখাস্ত) in the language of the court, usually Bangla, or, if the user acts for the other side, an objection to the other side's application.

1. Establish the case and court, the step fixed for the date, how many adjournments each side has already had, and the genuine reason for time (illness with a medical certificate, a document awaited from an office, change of advocate, a related proceeding).
2. Read Order XVII of the Code of Civil Procedure (adjournments and costs) on the Laws of Bangladesh, or the criminal-procedure provision for a criminal case.
3. Draft briefly: case title; the step fixed; the reason, specific and supported; the time asked for; an offer to pay costs if the court thinks fit; verification. Do not invent a reason or attach a document that does not exist.
4. For an objection: the adjournment history in a short table (date, party, reason, order), the prejudice of further delay, and a prayer that no further adjournment be allowed or that costs be imposed.
5. Generate a Word document.
