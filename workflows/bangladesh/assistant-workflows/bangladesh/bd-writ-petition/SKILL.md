# Writ Petition (Article 102)

Draft a writ petition for the High Court Division under Article 102 of the Constitution, in English unless the user asks for Bangla.

1. From the user's message and documents, establish: the petitioner and why they are a "person aggrieved"; each respondent (the Government through the secretary of the ministry concerned, the authority, the officer); the impugned order, action or omission and its date; the relief (certiorari, mandamus, prohibition, habeas corpus, quo warranto); any interim relief; and any alternative remedy or delay that must be explained. Ask for what is missing.
2. Read Article 102 and any statute the impugned act was made under with the Laws of Bangladesh tools. Do not cite a case unless it comes from a document the user gave you or a source you read.
3. Use the fixed structure:
   - cause title: "IN THE SUPREME COURT OF BANGLADESH, HIGH COURT DIVISION (SPECIAL ORIGINAL JURISDICTION)", "WRIT PETITION NO. ____ OF 20__", the parties with addresses, and "VERSUS";
   - "IN THE MATTER OF" recitals: the application under Article 102, the impugned act, and the law it concerns;
   - the statement of facts in numbered paragraphs, each tied to an annexure ("a copy of the order dated … is annexed as Annexure-A");
   - "GROUNDS", numbered, each a single legal point (without lawful authority, violation of fundamental rights, natural justice, mala fides, unreasonableness);
   - why no equally efficacious remedy exists, and any explanation of delay;
   - the prayer for a Rule Nisi calling on the respondents to show cause, with the interim relief sought;
   - the affidavit of the petitioner (or authorised representative) and the advocate's certificate.
4. Generate it as a Word document, and list the annexures the advocate must collect and paginate.
