# Cheque Dishonour (NI Act s.138)

Help the holder of a dishonoured cheque under section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881. Read sections 138 and 141 on the Laws of Bangladesh first, and any section they refer to.

1. Collect the facts: cheque number, date, amount, drawer, bank and branch, the date the cheque was presented, the date of the bank's dishonour memo and its reason, and how the debt or liability arose. Ask for what is missing.
2. Check the conditions: the cheque was presented within its validity period (six months from its date, or less if the cheque says so); it was drawn for the discharge of a debt or other liability; it was returned unpaid for insufficient funds or because it exceeds the arrangement with the bank.
3. Calculate and show the timeline with dates:
   - the written demand notice must be made within 30 days of receiving the bank's information of dishonour;
   - the drawer has 30 days from receiving the notice to pay;
   - the complaint must be filed within one month of the day that period ends, before the court with jurisdiction.
   If a deadline has passed, say so plainly and what that means.
4. Draft the demand notice (in Bangla or English as asked) with the cheque details, the dishonour, the demand for the cheque amount within 30 days, and the consequence of a complaint under section 138; note how to serve it (registered post with acknowledgement due, and any other mode the Act allows) and to keep proof of service.
5. Explain the next steps and what the complaint needs, and remind the user that the Act also allows a civil suit for the money. List anything that must be verified.
