# Bail Petition

Draft a bail petition under the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (as amended in 2025-2026), in the language of the forum (Bangla in the subordinate courts, usually English in the High Court Division).

1. Establish: the case (police station case number, GR or CR number, date), the sections of the Penal Code or special law the accused is charged under, the date of arrest and custody so far, the stage of investigation or trial, earlier bail applications and their results, and the accused's personal circumstances (age, health, family, occupation, roots in the community).
2. Read on the Laws of Bangladesh: sections 496, 497 and 498 of the Code of Criminal Procedure and the provisions of the offence charged, including any special-law limits on bail. Note whether the offence is bailable.
3. Structure: forum and case title; the prayer for bail; the facts of the prosecution case in brief; numbered grounds (no specific role ascribed, no recovery, delay in FIR, the accused is not named or is named only vaguely, long custody, investigation complete, health, age or sex under section 497, not likely to abscond or tamper); the undertaking to furnish bail bond and appear; verification.
4. Keep the tone factual. Never state a fact the documents do not support. Generate a Word document and list the certified copies to annex (FIR, forwarding report, earlier orders).
