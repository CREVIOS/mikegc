# Legal Translation (Bangla ⇄ English)

Translate the attached document, or the passage the user gives, between Bangla and English.

1. Translate the meaning, not word by word, in the register of Bangladeshi legal drafting. Bangla must be standard Bangladeshi Bangla (প্রমিত বাংলা) with Bangla Academy spelling, written in Unicode, with the dari (।) and Bangla digits in Bangla prose.
2. Keep the structure exactly: the same clause numbers, headings, defined terms, schedules and cross-references. Keep proper names, company names, addresses, amounts and dates accurate; write amounts in figures and words in both languages (Tk 1,25,000 / ৳ ১,২৫,০০০/-; Taka one lakh twenty-five thousand / এক লক্ষ পঁচিশ হাজার টাকা).
3. Use the established equivalents: plaintiff বাদী, defendant বিবাদী, plaint আরজি, written statement লিখিত জবাব, affidavit হলফনামা, power of attorney আমমোক্তারনামা, deed দলিল, schedule তফসিল, section ধারা, sub-section উপধারা, clause দফা, injunction নিষেধাজ্ঞা, arbitration সালিশ, indemnity ক্ষতিপূরণের দায়, notwithstanding anything contained in এতে যাহা কিছুই থাকুক না কেন. When a term has no settled equivalent, translate it and give the original in brackets the first time.
4. Statute names: give the official Bangla or English short title as the Laws of Bangladesh publishes it; check it with bd_law_search when unsure.
5. Do not add, drop or soften obligations. If the source is ambiguous or seems wrong, translate it faithfully and add a translator's note at the end.
6. Produce the translation as a Word document (or side by side in two columns if the user asks), and say that a certified translation for a court or registry needs the translator's or advocate's certificate.
