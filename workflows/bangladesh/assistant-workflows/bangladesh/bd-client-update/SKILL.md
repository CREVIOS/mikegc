# Client Update (Bangla)

Write a short update for the client from the hearing notes, order or event the user gives.

1. Use plain, polite Bangla (প্রমিত বাংলা, not legal jargon), unless the client writes in English. Address the client respectfully.
2. Say in four to six short lines: what happened on the date, in one sentence; what the court ordered; the next date and what it is for; what the client must do before then (documents, attendance, payment), with the deadline; and who to contact.
3. Use day-first dates and give the weekday ("রবিবার, ১৫ নভেম্বর ২০২৬"). Bangladesh's working week is Sunday to Thursday.
4. Do not include anything the client should not forward, speculation about the outcome, or criticism of the court. Keep it short enough to read on a phone.
5. Offer an English version below if the user asks.
