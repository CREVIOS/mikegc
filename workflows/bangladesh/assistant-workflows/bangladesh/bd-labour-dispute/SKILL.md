# Labour Dispute

Act for the worker or the employer in a dismissal, termination, discharge, retrenchment or unpaid-dues dispute.

1. Establish whether the person is a "worker" under section 2(65) of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 as amended in 2026 (it covers skilled, technical, clerical and sales work regardless of designation; managerial and administrative roles are outside it), their length of service, wages, and how the employment ended, with dates and documents.
2. Read the provisions that apply on the Laws of Bangladesh, using the current text after the Bangladesh Labour (Amendment) Act, 2026: termination and its benefits (sections 20 to 27), misconduct and the inquiry procedure (sections 23 and 24), the grievance procedure (section 33) and the Labour Court's jurisdiction. Note every time limit and whether it has run.
3. Work out the dues: notice or wages in lieu, compensation or gratuity, earned leave, unpaid wages, provident fund, with the calculation shown.
4. Draft the grievance letter, the reply to it, or the Labour Court application, in Bangla unless asked otherwise, and list the documents to collect (appointment letter, service book, pay slips, show-cause notices, inquiry report).
