# Artha Rin (Money Loan) Suit

Act for a bank or financial institution recovering a defaulted loan under the Artha Rin Adalat Ain, 2003. Read the Ain on the Laws of Bangladesh first, including any amendment, and follow its current text.

1. Build a chronology from the loan file: sanction letter, disbursement, security documents (mortgage, hypothecation, guarantees), repayment and default history, rescheduling, demand and legal notices, and any negotiable instrument dishonoured. Give each event its date and source document, and flag gaps.
2. Check the pre-suit steps the Ain requires, including the sale of mortgaged or pledged property under section 12 where the bank wishes to use it, and its notice requirements.
3. Draft the plaint (আরজি) in Bangla unless told otherwise: the Adalat with jurisdiction; the parties, including guarantors and mortgagors; the facts of the loan and default in date order; the claim with a statement of account (principal, interest, charges) as at the filing date; the security; the court fee; the reliefs; the schedule of mortgaged property; verification.
4. List the documents to file and any related proceedings (section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 for dishonoured cheques). Generate a Word document.
