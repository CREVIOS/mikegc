# Affidavit-in-Chief (Order XVIII r.4A)

Under the Code of Civil Procedure (Amendment) Act, 2026, a witness's examination-in-chief in a civil suit is given by affidavit under Order XVIII rule 4A and served on the other side before filing; cross-examination remains oral. Read Order XVIII on the Laws of Bangladesh first and follow its current text.

1. Establish the suit (court, suit number, parties), the witness and their relationship to the case, and the facts the witness can prove from their own knowledge. Use the plaint or written statement and the documents the user provides.
2. Draft in the language of the suit (usually Bangla in the subordinate courts):
   - title of the suit and "হলফনামা মূলে জবানবন্দি" / "Affidavit-in-chief of PW-1";
   - the deponent's particulars (name, father's or husband's name, age, address, NID);
   - numbered paragraphs, in date order, each stating one fact the witness knows personally, and saying how they know it; distinguish what is from records;
   - each document the witness proves, with its exhibit number placeholder ("Exhibit-1");
   - the verification and the commissioner's or notary's attestation.
3. Do not put argument, law or hearsay in the affidavit. Flag any paragraph that relies on a document not yet produced or a fact the witness cannot speak to.
4. Generate a Word document and a short list of exhibits to be marked.
