# Bangladesh corporate examples

Five foreign-drafted documents and the markup a Bangladeshi lawyer makes to each. The `-redline.docx` files carry real Word tracked changes made by Spectre's edit engine (author "Spectre"): open one in Word and use Review → Accept or Reject. Every change cites the provision it rests on. Each file is regenerated by `backend/scripts/build-bangladesh-examples.ts`, and `backend/src/lib/bangladesh/demo/__tests__/useCases.test.ts` checks that every edit applies and lands on word boundaries.

These are demonstrations, not advice: confirm the points listed under "To confirm" before using any wording.

## 1. Term facility agreement with an offshore lender

Acting for the Bangladeshi borrower. Offshore and DFI lenders lend to Bangladeshi companies on English-law LMA-style documents; local counsel marks up the approvals, tax and security provisions. Invest Bangladesh replaced BIDA from 20 August 2026, and Bangladesh Bank consolidated its foreign-exchange rules on loans on 2 September 2026.

| ~~4% per annum above the rate otherwise applicable, compounded monthly~~ **2% per annum above the rate otherwise applicable, calculated on a simple (non-co…** | Increased interest from default may be a penalty, recoverable only as reasonable compensation. | Contract Act, 1872, s.74 and its Explanation |
| **The Borrower shall make any Tax Deduction required under section 119 of the Inco…** | Interest paid abroad bears withholding tax; treaty relief needs the lender's residence certificate and an NBR certificate. | Income Tax Act, 2023, ss.119 and 145 |

**Cover note**

The representation on authorisations now names Invest Bangladesh, which replaced BIDA from 20 August 2026, and Bangladesh Bank's consolidated foreign-exchange rules of 2 September 2026: without those authorisations both drawdown and repayment remittances are at risk. The security covenant now follows the 21-day RJSC filing period in section 159 of the Companies Act, 1994, because a charge filed late is void against a liquidator, and the registration and stamping steps under the Registration Act and the Stamp Act. Default interest is a simple 2% margin: section 74 of the Contract Act treats increased interest on default as a possible penalty. The tax clause keeps the gross-up but requires the Lender's help with treaty relief.

**To confirm**

- Stamp duty on the mortgage and other security at the current Finance Act rates (drafted "at the rate then applicable").
- Whether the lender's jurisdiction has a double taxation agreement with Bangladesh and the reduced rate.
- The exact Schedule 2 list of Invest Bangladesh and Bangladesh Bank authorisations for this borrowing.

## 2. Telecom vendor master services agreement

Acting for the Bangladeshi telecom operator (customer). Operators and banks buy network equipment, software and cloud services from foreign vendors on vendor paper. Every payment raises VAT and income tax deduction at source, and the 2026 data protection law limits where personal data may be held.

Files: [draft](02-vendor-msa-draft.docx) · [redline](02-vendor-msa-redline.docx)

| Clause change | Why | Provision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~pay all invoiced amounts in full without set-off, counterclaim or deduction of a…~~ **(a) deduct VAT at source under section 49 of the Value Added Tax and Supplementa…** | The customer is jointly liable for VAT it fails to deduct and loses the expense deduction if it fails to deduct income tax. | VAT and Supplementary Duty Act, 2012, s.49; Income Tax Act, 2023, ss.89, 90, 119 and 55 |
| ~~and its Affiliates and Subprocessors may store, process and access Customer Data…~~ **shall store and process Customer Data that is confidential or restricted persona…** | Confidential and restricted personal data must be held in Bangladesh, and other transfers need a lawful basis and an adequate destination. | Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 (data classification and cross-border transfer provisions) |
| ~~Any claim arising under this Agreement must be brought within twelve (12) months…~~ **Each Party shall notify the other of any claim within a reasonable time after be…** | A clause limiting the time to enforce rights in the ordinary courts is void. | Contract Act, 1872, s.28 |
| **Supplier shall ensure that all Equipment imported for delivery is covered by a v…** | Telecom equipment needs BTRC vendor enlistment and a no-objection certificate for each shipment. | Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission import requirements |

**Cover note**

The tax clause now has you deduct VAT at source and income tax at source as the law requires, with no gross-up: under the VAT and Supplementary Duty Act, 2012 you are jointly liable for VAT you fail to deduct, and under the Income Tax Act, 2023 the expense is disallowed if tax is not deducted. The data clause keeps confidential and restricted personal data, and all subscriber data, in Bangladesh, as the 2026 data protection law requires. The 12-month claims bar is deleted because section 28 of the Contract Act makes it void. BTRC import approvals are now the Supplier's responsibility.

**To confirm**

- VAT and income tax deduction rates for each service line (drafted "at the rate then applicable").
- Section numbers of the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 as gazetted, and any regulations on transfer destinations.

## 3. Appointment letter for a multinational's Dhaka subsidiary

Acting for the employer. Group templates drafted abroad meet the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 as amended in 2026, whose definition of "worker" covers skilled, technical, clerical and sales staff whatever their designation; only managerial and administrative roles are outside it.

Files: [draft](03-appointment-letter-draft.docx) · [redline](03-appointment-letter-redline.docx)

| Clause change | Why | Provision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~six (6) months, which the Company may extend at its sole discretion~~ **the period that applies to your role under section 4 of the Bangladesh Labour Ac…** | Probation is six months for clerical work and three months for other workers, and may be extended only as the Act allows. | Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, s.4 |
| ~~Your employment is at will and may be terminated by either party at any time by …~~ **After confirmation, the Company may terminate your employment otherwise than by …** | A permanent monthly-rated worker is entitled to 120 days' notice and service-based compensation; resignation needs 60 days' notice. | Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006, ss.26 and 27 |
| ~~For twelve (12) months after the termination of your employment, you shall not b…~~ **During your employment, including any notice period, you shall not without the C…** | A restraint on working after employment ends is void; in-employment duties and confidentiality are enforceable. | Contract Act, 1872, s.27 |
| ~~the Republic of Singapore~~ **Bangladesh. Nothing in it reduces any right or benefit to which you are entitled…** | The employee works in Bangladesh, where the Labour Act's minimum standards apply. | Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 |

**Cover note**

A Senior Sales Executive is very likely a "worker" under the 2026 definition, whatever the title. For workers, "at will" termination on 30 days' notice is not lawful: section 26 requires 120 days' notice (or wages in lieu) and compensation based on service, and section 27 gives a 60-day resignation period. Probation now follows section 4. The post-employment non-compete is replaced with an in-employment restriction and continuing confidentiality, because section 27 of the Contract Act makes a post-employment restraint void. Governing law is Bangladeshi.

**To confirm**

- Whether the role is genuinely managerial or administrative (outside the Labour Act's definition of worker), which changes the termination terms.
- The Company's permanent headcount, which decides provident fund and workers' participation fund obligations under the 2026 amendments.

## 4. Exclusive distribution agreement with a foreign principal

Acting for the Bangladeshi distributor. Foreign FMCG, pharmaceutical and industrial brands enter Bangladesh through local distributors on the principal's template, which routinely carries post-term non-competes, termination for convenience, resale price control and time bars.

Files: [draft](04-distribution-agreement-draft.docx) · [redline](04-distribution-agreement-redline.docx)

| Clause change | Why | Provision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~~, Distributor shall pay Principal, as liquidated damages, an amount equal to 100…~~ **in two consecutive Contract Years, Principal's sole remedy shall be to make Dist…** | A 100% shortfall payment is a penalty, recoverable only as reasonable compensation. | Contract Act, 1872, s.74 |
| ~~Distributor shall resell the Products only at the prices set out in Principal's …~~ **Principal may issue recommended resale prices. Distributor is free to determine …** | Resale price maintenance is a listed anti-competitive practice. | Competition Act, 2012, s.15 |
| ~~ and for two (2) years thereafter, Distributor shall not directly or indirectly …~~ **, Distributor shall not directly or indirectly import or distribute in the Terri…** | A restraint after the agreement ends is void; exclusivity during the term is kept. | Contract Act, 1872, s.27 |
| ~~Principal may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on thirty (30…~~ **Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on not less than six (…** | Thirty days with no compensation leaves the distributor with stranded stock and investment; reasonable notice and a buy-back are standard. | Contract Act, 1872, ss.205 and 206 (by analogy for a buy-sell distributor) |
| ~~No claim may be brought more than six (6) months after it arose~~ **Nothing in this Clause prevents either Party from seeking interim or conservator…** | The six-month time bar is void, and the courts' power to grant interim relief for a foreign-seated arbitration is unsettled, so it is preserved expressly. | Contract Act, 1872, s.28; Arbitration Act, 2001, ss.3, 7A and 45 |

**Cover note**

The two-year post-term non-compete is void under section 27 of the Contract Act, so only in-term exclusivity is kept. The 100% shortfall payment would be cut down to reasonable compensation under section 74, and resale price control is an anti-competitive practice listed in section 15 of the Competition Act, 2012; both are replaced. Termination for convenience now needs six months' notice with a stock buy-back. SIAC arbitration stays, the six-month time bar is removed (void under section 28), and access to Bangladeshi courts for interim relief is preserved.

**To confirm**

- Whether the arrangement is a true agency (sections 205 and 206 of the Contract Act apply directly) or buy-sell distribution.
- Indenting registration and Bangladesh Bank requirements if the Distributor also acts as indenting agent.

## 5. Shareholders' agreement with a foreign investor

Acting for the Bangladeshi company and its promoters. Foreign investors take minority stakes in Bangladeshi private companies under shareholders' agreements. Bangladesh Bank's FEID Circular No. 01 of 8 March 2026 requires non-resident share transfers to be at fair value, with valuation and reporting rules.

Files: [draft](05-shareholders-agreement-draft.docx) · [redline](05-shareholders-agreement-redline.docx)

| Clause change | Why | Provision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **, provided that the transfer is made at fair value as Bangladesh Bank regulation…** | Non-resident share transfers must be at fair value and are recorded by Form 117; the transfer instrument bears stamp duty. | Bangladesh Bank FEID Circular No. 01 (8 March 2026); Companies Act, 1994; Stamp Act, 1899 |
| ~~Subscription Price plus an internal rate of return of 15% per annum, payable in …~~ **fair value of the Investor Shares on the exercise date, determined by an indepen…** | A guaranteed-return price may depart from fair value, so the authorised dealer could refuse repatriation without Bangladesh Bank approval. | Bangladesh Bank FEID Circular No. 01 (8 March 2026); Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947 |
| ~~shall not, for three (3) years after ceasing to hold Shares, engage in any busin…~~ **who sells Shares to the Investor under this Agreement shall not, for two (2) yea…** | A worldwide restraint is void; only a restraint tied to the sale of goodwill, within local limits, can be saved. | Contract Act, 1872, s.27, Exception 1 |
| ~~In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the Articles, this Agree…~~ **Within thirty (30) days after Completion, the Shareholders shall pass a special …** | Investor protections bind the company only once they are in its Articles; a contract between shareholders does not bind the company's statutory powers. | Companies Act, 1994 |

**Cover note**

The 15% guaranteed-return put conflicts with Bangladesh Bank's fair-value rule for non-resident share transfers (FEID Circular No. 01 of 8 March 2026), so repatriation could be refused or delayed; it is now a fair-value put paid in Taka and repatriated through an authorised dealer. Affiliate transfers must also be at fair value, recorded on Form 117 and stamped. The investor's protections bind the Company only once they are in the Articles, so the Shareholders now covenant to adopt and file them. The worldwide promoter non-compete is narrowed to the goodwill-sale exception in section 27 of the Contract Act.

**To confirm**

- Stamp duty on the share transfer at the current rate (reported as 1.5% of consideration for unlisted shares; drafted "at the rate then applicable").
- Whether Invest Bangladesh clearance is needed for this investment.
